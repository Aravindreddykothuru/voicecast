What these sentences are for

Our dubbing voice mispronounces a word when that word is the entire utterance — a lone “Okay” or “No” comes out as a different word. So a short line is spoken after one of these sentences, and the sentence is then cut away. Viewers never hear it. It still matters: an awkward or ungrammatical sentence can make the voice pronounce the line after it oddly. Until a fluent speaker signs a sheet, that language is recorded as machine-checked only.
